## Situation

A data migration programme had to move legacy Talend ETL jobs to Databricks on Azure, and a team of 7 engineers needed a shared approach before starting.

## Task

Own the technical approach: how jobs are inventoried, re-implemented, validated and cut over, while keeping delivery on schedule.

## Action

I built a job inventory, agreed re-implementation patterns in Python and PySpark with the team, set code review and engineering standards, and introduced AI agents and reusable skills to speed up job analysis, conversion, testing and documentation.
